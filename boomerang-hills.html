About Boomerang Hills

Boomerang Hills, formerly Boomerang Farm, golf course and venue located in Mudgeeraba with the occasional aesthetic historical farm buildings

Check hours, junior pricing, and hire before you visit. Putt-putt has its own fees, so therefore don't assume your golf booking will include it.
