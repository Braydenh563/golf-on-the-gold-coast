Gold Coast driving ranges

Pick a venue to see its full guide.

  • Topgolf Gold Coast

    Play tracked target focused semi-virtual games at Topgolf Gold Coast. Bays fit up to six players and 8 people. Note however, that younger guests must be under adult-supervision.

    Explore Topgolf ➜

  • KDV Sport, Carrara

    Practise undercover at KDV Sport in Carrara. Clubs are available for hire with availability reaching late into the night until 10pm.

    Explore KDV Sport ➜

  • Emerald Lakes Golf Club

    Emerald Lakes Golf Club has a mix of both open and undercover bays, neighbouring KDV Sport while remaining an alternative venue. It doubles as both a high-end randge and golf course. Check its official range page before you visit.
