About Topgolf Gold Coast

Topgolf Gold Coast is a golf entertainment venue in Oxenford, sititng between Outback Spectacular and Warner Brothers Movie World. Players hit balls towards targets, with integrated technology tracking shots and scores. Bays accommodate up to six players, and groups of up to 8 people.

No previous golf experience is required, and complimentary clubs are available. Check the hourly rate before booking and note the cost of new-player membership fees ($5 lifetime). Visitors aged 17 or younger need a responsible adult. Additional restrictions apply after 5pm.
