About Parkwood Golf Course

Parkwood Golf Course is just a part of Parkwood Village and everything it has to offer. Its complete 18-hole course layout includes lush fairways, quick greens and small and water bodies. Bookings cna be made online through their website.

If you are new to golf, make sure to ask about a junior clinic before booking a round/game. Check the pricing and fees, as well as equipment hire and junior requirements before visiting. An annual junior membership is also available.
