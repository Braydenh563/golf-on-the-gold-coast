About KDV Sport

KDV Sport is a large and general sporting facility in Carrara which comes with a driving range, golf course, and even coaching programmes. It features multiple bays with both small and large buckets of balls available for practice.

Walk-in games are easy to aquire without the stress of a pre-booking, as well as hire clubs through reception. The range operates from 7am to 10pm. Make sure to ask about junior supervision and current bucket prices before your first visit.
